Sponsor Jottit

Why sponsor

Jottit is free to use and not a business. That's deliberate. It means nobody has to pay, and it also means there's no revenue to fund the work.

If you want to help keep Jottit independent and honest, sponsorship is how.

What it pays for

Hosting, domains, and the unglamorous infrastructure that keeps pages online. Time to fix bugs, add the small handful of features that still matter, and keep the thing maintained over the long term.

Nothing exotic. Just the cost of keeping a small, careful tool alive on the open web.

What doesn't change

Jottit's promises hold regardless of who sponsors: no ads, no tracking, no algorithmic feeds, no AI writing assistance, no paywalls, no sponsored content, no tiered features. Sponsors are thanked, not given special treatment.

How to sponsor

The easiest way is GitHub Sponsors. Any amount helps, and you can cancel anytime.

If GitHub Sponsors doesn't work for you — company procurement, different payment method, one-off support — email me and we'll figure something out.

Thank you, Paul Graham. And thank you, whoever you are, for reading this far.
