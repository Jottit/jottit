About Jottit

What

Jottit is a page on the web. You type markdown, pick a URL, and you have a page. No account. No algorithm deciding who reads it. No dashboard between you and your words.

That was the whole idea in 2007. It's still the whole idea.

A short history

2005

Aaron Swartz and Simon Carstensen meet through Y Combinator's first batch. They build Infogami together, a tool for making websites easy.

2007

The original Jottit ships. A textarea, a save button, a URL. Thousands of tiny sites get made.

2013

After Aaron's death, the site is kept running out of respect. It quietly falls offline some years later. The domain stays. The idea stays.

2026

Simon rebuilds Jottit from scratch. Same idea, same name. The stack is new; the promise is the same. Your words, your URL, yours.

Elsewhere

To contact the project, see talk to us. If you'd like to help keep Jottit independent, there's a sponsor page. The source is on GitHub under AGPL.

Open source (AGPL). Funded by Paul Graham. Built in memory of Aaron Swartz.
